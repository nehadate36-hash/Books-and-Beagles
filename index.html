About Us

At Books and Beagles, we believe every book has the power to inspire, educate, and transport us to new worlds. Our bookstore is a welcoming space for readers of all ages to discover new authors, revisit timeless classics, and connect with ideas that spark curiosity and imagination. Whether you're searching for your next great read, a thoughtful gift, or simply a quiet place to explore, Books and Beagles is dedicated to fostering a love of reading and creating a community where stories bring people together.

Our Services

1. Books & Brew Recommendations ☕📚

This service helps customers choose both a book and a drink based on their interests, mood, or preferences. Employees are trained to recommend books and beverages that complement one another.

Examples

  • Mystery novel + Dark Roast Coffee
  • Romance novel + Caramel Latte
  • Fantasy novel + Chai Tea
  • Historical fiction + Hot Chocolate

2. Cozy Reading & Study Space 📖

Books and Beagles offers a comfortable environment where customers can relax, read, study, work, or meet friends while enjoying coffee and snacks.

Features

  • Comfortable chairs and couches
  • Quiet reading corners
  • Free Wi-Fi
  • Charging stations
  • Large tables for studying or group work
  • Warm lighting and inviting décor

3. Book Clubs & Community Events 🎉

Books and Beagles hosts events that bring readers and community members together

Types of Events

  • Monthly Book Clubs
  • Author Visits and Book Signings
  • Poetry and Open Mic Nights
  • Writing Workshops
  • Children's Story Time
